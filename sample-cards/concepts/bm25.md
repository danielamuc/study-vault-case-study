# BM25

**In short:** A ranking function for lexical search that improves TF-IDF with term-frequency saturation and document-length normalisation.

**In the Summer Workshop (Weekend 5):** Deck 2: two knobs, k (usually written k1) for term-frequency saturation, rule of thumb 1.2 to 2.0, and b for length normalisation, 0.75. A larger b penalises long documents more. Its scores (for example 28.1) are unbounded and not comparable with cosine scores, which is why hybrid search fuses ranks.
