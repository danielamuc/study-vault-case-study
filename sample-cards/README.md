# Sample cards

Three cards from the vault, exactly as they look after `tools/vault.py build`. Everything above the
`generated` marker is written by hand (by me or by an agent under my rules); everything below it is
computed by the inference step.

- [projects/compliance-rag.md](projects/compliance-rag.md): one of my projects, the concepts it applies, and what I built and measured.
- [concepts/hybrid-search.md](concepts/hybrid-search.md): a concept taught in two courses and applied in two projects.
- [concepts/bm25.md](concepts/bm25.md): a concept with its broader chain and prerequisites.

Links like `[[lexical-retrieval]]` point to cards that are not in this repository. Source paths start
with a root name from `roots.json` (see [roots.example.json](roots.example.json)), so the vault works
on any computer where the raw files live in a different place.
